Industry Challenge

The Dual-Frontier Dilemma: Why Generic ERPs Fail

Cross-border trading enterprises in Ethiopia operate across two incompatible worlds with severe compliance penalties.

Agri-Export Friction
WH1 Modjo Operations
  • ECX lot grading, moisture loss & variable tare weight
  • Destoning & hulling shrinkage not tracked in standard ERPs
  • Complex export documentation: Phytosanitary & FX permits
  • Result: massive unallocated inventory bleed & audit penalties
Pharma & Vet Perils
WH2 & WH3 Distribution
  • Standard FIFO dispatch sends near-expired drugs to clinics
  • Zero automated quarantine locks on EFDA flagged lots
  • Multi-currency landed costing (freight, duty, port demurrage)
  • Result: high inventory write-downs and regulatory friction
Statutory Tax Disconnect
Finance & Accounting
  • Cloud ERPs isolate finance from existing Peachtree systems
  • Failure to handle 2% Goods WHT and 30% Service WHT
  • Inability to perform split COA cost allocations per voucher
  • Result: painful month-end manual Excel reconciliations

WH1 Agricultural Engine

From ECX Intake to Djibouti Port: Zero Discrepancy

Precision toll processing, dynamic shrinkage calculation, and automated statutory receiving vouchers.

The End-to-End Commodity Processing Pipeline
Automated Yield & Tare Reconciliation
  • 1. Intake Lab Recording: Truck tare weight, sample moisture test %, impurity level, and supplier consignment ID captured immediately.
  • 2. Multi-Stage Mechanical Processing: Destoning, gravity separation, screen grading, and optical sorting tracks clean output vs rejects.
  • 3. Dynamic Shrinkage Analytics: Automatic formula reconciliation between raw mass and final export bag count, preventing unauthorized stock diversion.
  • 4. Automated Toll Fee Engine: Instant billing based on tiered rates (ETB or USD per quintal) with instant customer invoice generation.
Supplier Quality Scorecards
Data-Driven Sourcing

Evaluates suppliers and cooperatives based on raw grain yield, foreign matter %, and moisture variance. Enforces automated price adjustments for subpar deliveries.

Statutory Model 19 & Bin Cards
Audit-Proof Documentation

Generates official Ethiopian Model 19 Goods Receiving Vouchers and electronic Bin Cards compliant with national trade inspectorates.

Healthcare Precision

WH2 & WH3 Pharma Governance: Zero Expired Stock

Strict First-Expired, First-Out (FEFO) algorithms, automated expiry radars, and instant quarantine isolation.

Strict FEFO Enforcement
Algorithmic Dispatch Logic
  • Order reservation automatically locks the earliest valid expiry lot.
  • Warehouse pick lists strictly sequence older valid batches first.
  • Prevents older stock from getting trapped behind new deliveries.
  • Saves tens of thousands of dollars in spoiled medicine write-offs.
Triple-Tier Expiry Radar
Proactive Window Alerts
  • 90 Days: Commercial warning to trigger special promotional campaigns.
  • 60 Days: Restricts dispatches to short-lead local clinics only.
  • 30 Days: Hard block; dispatches prohibited without Superadmin authorization.
One-Click Quarantine Matrix
EFDA Regulatory Safety
  • Instant lockdown on batches flagged for temperature excursions or lab holds.
  • Stock is immediately subtracted from ATP (Available-To-Promise).
  • Full digital audit log of isolation reason, date, and approving officer.
